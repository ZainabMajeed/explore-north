Essential Travel Tips

Plan smart for a safe and unforgettable mountain adventure

Best Time to Visit

May to October is ideal for pleasant weather and open roads. Visit in October for magical autumn colors.

Pack Warm Layers

Even in summer, mountain nights get chilly. Always carry a windproof jacket, hiking shoes, and sunglasses.

Cash & Connectivity

ATMs can be limited in remote valleys. Carry sufficient cash and keep an SCOM SIM for reliable network.
